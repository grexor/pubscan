Find scientific collaborators and the leading researchers of a field

Type a research topic and pubScan lists the authors who publish most on it. One click opens the collaboration network of any of them: their co-authors, their groups and their papers.

Opens the best matching of 4,700 topics and subfields, or of 65,000 keywords. Or browse all topics by field.

How it works

1

Search a topic

Use the box above, or the search box of pubScan itself, which suggests topics and keywords while you type.

2

Read the ranking

The 100 authors with the most publications on the topic, with their ORCID iD and the year of their newest paper on it. Set “active since” to keep only researchers who still publish in the field.

3

Open a network

Click a name to see who that researcher works with. Clusters reveal the groups around them, and the publication list shows their affiliation and papers.

From a ranking to a collaborator

Who works with whom

The network of a leading author shows the people around them. Co-authors who publish together form colored clusters: often the lab, a partner institute, or a long-running consortium.

Where they are

The publication list of every author names the main affiliation, taken from their most recent publications, so you can see which institutions are active in the field.

What else they do

The Topics panel lists the other topics an author publishes on, and marks those where the author is among the top 100. Useful to find someone who bridges two fields.

Who is active now

Restrict a network to recent years to see current collaborations, and use “active since” on a topic list to leave out authors who have moved on.

The rankings count publications, not quality or impact, and include only authors with an ORCID iD. Read them as a map of who is active in a field, not as a league table. More about the data.
